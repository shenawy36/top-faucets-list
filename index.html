What are Crypto Faucet & PTC Sites?

A Crypto Faucet is a specialized website or application that distributes tiny fractions of cryptocurrencies (such as Bitcoin, Solana, or Ethereum) as a reward for completing simple, straightforward tasks. The name "faucet" is a metaphor: just as a leaky faucet drips water drop by drop, these platforms drip small amounts of digital assets into your wallet over time.

How do they work?

Webmasters generate revenue by displaying advertisements, offerwalls, or promotional videos on their websites. A portion of this ad revenue is then shared with the users who interact with the platform. Tasks usually involve solving a Captcha (to prove you are human), clicking a "Claim" button, watching a brief video, or navigating a shortlink.

What about PTC (Paid-To-Click)?

PTC platforms operate on a similar concept but focus entirely on advertising. Advertisers pay the PTC network to get traffic to their own websites, and the network passes a cut of that payment to you for viewing the ad for a specific duration (usually 5 to 30 seconds). It is one of the easiest ways for beginners to accumulate cryptocurrency without any initial investment or technical trading knowledge.
